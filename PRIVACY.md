# Privacy

Translate & Fit for Illustrator runs only on your computer.

- It does not connect to the internet. The only exception is the Help link in the panel, which opens this page in your browser when you click it.
- It does not collect, send or store personal data.
- It reads and writes files only where you choose: the XLIFF and map files next to your document, and your settings (language codes and limits) in your user folder.
- It stores a copy of the map and, in the trial, a usage count inside the Illustrator document itself.

Purchases on Adobe Exchange are handled by Adobe and its payment provider. Their privacy policies apply to that information. If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.
